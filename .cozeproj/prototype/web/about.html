01 — The Beginning

The story starts with a question that had no good answer

Why does serious strength training demand an entire room, a wall of weights, and a tolerance for noise?

For over a decade, the engineering team behind SENSOL — based in Shanghai and operating under Thunderbird Tech Limited — worked quietly at the intersection of robotics, motor control, and fitness biomechanics.

Their goal was deceptively simple: compress the function of an entire gym into a single piece of furniture. Not a multi-station cable machine. Not a foldable bench with plates. Something fundamentally new.

That vision became the Digital Weight System — a motor-driven resistance engine controlled by software, replacing iron plates with precise, instant, silent load. It was a concept that had been attempted before, but never in a form this compact, this quiet, and this precise.

02 — The Journey

2023

Going Public

After years of prototyping and iteration, SENSOL made its public debut. The first product, the TB02 Smart Fitness Bench, launched simultaneously on Kickstarter and Indiegogo. The response was immediate — backers from the United States, Europe, and Asia pledged support, drawn to the promise of an all-in-one smart gym that could replace racks of equipment with a single sleek bench.

That same month, SENSOL was featured on Product Hunt, earning recognition as one of the most innovative new products in the smart home fitness space.

HK$550K Raised 2 Platforms
2024

Shipping, Refining, Listening

By January 2024, all crowdfunding units had been shipped. Deliveries reached backers across the western and eastern United States, Europe, and Asia. But shipping was only the beginning.

The real work started when people started training on SENSOL in their homes — and started telling us what worked, what confused them, and what they wished the system could do. That feedback drove rapid iteration. The SENSOL Smart App evolved through multiple updates, adding Bluetooth direct control, weight unit switching, custom workout creation, real-time performance analytics, and an expanding library of guided classes. The AI coaching engine became smarter with every session.

2025

Senzine: Where Movement Meets Thought

Beyond the hardware, SENSOL has always been about a philosophy of training. In 2025, we launched Senzine — SENSOL's internal publication. Each issue distills a single idea — control, resistance, stillness — into a tactile experience, designed to be felt as much as it is read.

Released in quiet intervals, Senzine carries fragments of movement philosophy, reflections on design, and moments of attention. It is not a catalogue. It is not a manual. It is an invitation to think differently about why you train.

2026

Crossing to Australia

As SENSOL's reputation grew, one market kept surfacing in community feedback, media inquiries, and direct customer requests: Australia. Australian fitness culture is distinct — it values quality, it values design, and it has zero patience for equipment that can't be delivered quickly or supported locally.

That's why Dute Fitness (Australia) Pty. Ltd. was established. Registered in Victoria in July 2026, Dute Fitness is the exclusive Australian distributor for SENSOL. Every unit sold through sensolfitness.com.au is covered by local warranty, supported by a local team, and shipped from a local warehouse.

03 — The Line

Three expressions. One philosophy.

The original TB02 proved that the concept worked. The next challenge was making SENSOL accessible to more people, at more fitness levels, in more spaces — from motor-driven precision to spring-based flow.

Smart Series

RS02

The refined dual-servo digital weight platform. Motor-driven, software-controlled, AI-enhanced. The foundation of the SENSOL training experience.

Smart Series

RS02 PRO

Premium features and expanded resistance programming. For those who demand the most from every rep — more data, more control, more precision.

Flex Series

FLEX AIR

Spring-based resistance in the same iconic form. The SENSOL design philosophy without the motor system — lighter, more accessible, Pilates-inspired.

Every product shares the same design DNA — compact, intentional, and built around the human body. From the motor-driven precision of the Smart Series to the spring-based flow of the Flex Series, the same conviction runs through every curve, every material, every mechanism.

04 — Growing in the US

From crowdfunding to retail shelves

SENSOL's presence in the US market grew steadily after the initial crowdfunding deliveries. The brand established sensolfitness.com as its direct-to-consumer channel, and in 2026 partnered with Vesta Fitness as its official retail partner — making SENSOL the first smart fitness bench available for hands-on trial and in-stock purchase through a US retailer.

By 2026, SENSOL had become a recognised name in the smart home gym category — listed alongside Tonal, Speediance, and Oxefit in major product roundups and buyer's guides.

The SENSOL Smart App continued to serve the North American user base with AI-driven coaching, a growing library of on-demand classes, and seamless integration across the entire product line.
