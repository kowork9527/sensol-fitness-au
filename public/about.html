01 — Our Story

Redefining home fitness

Sensol was founded in 2022 with a clear mission: to redefine home fitness through intelligent, space-efficient equipment that delivers studio-grade training without compromise.

The concept was built on a simple observation. Traditional home fitness equipment forced users to choose between performance and practicality — machines were either effective but bulky, or compact but limited. Sensol set out to eliminate that trade-off.

After more than ten years of engineering and prototyping, the first Sensol machine combined digital weight technology, robotic arms with 145-degree range of motion, and a compact form factor that folds for storage. The result is a product line that delivers up to 120 kg of resistance, supports over 200 movements across three training modes, and fits into real living spaces.

02 — Public Launch

From concept to community

In September 2023, Sensol launched simultaneously on Kickstarter and Indiegogo. The Kickstarter campaign reached 533% of its funding goal, drawing supporters from the United States, Canada, Europe, and the Asia-Pacific region.

Later that year, Sensol was featured on Product Hunt, expanding its reach within the global technology and design community.

By early 2024, all backer orders had been fulfilled across the US, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, with machines placed in real homes rather than test environments.

03 — The Product Line

Two series. One standard.

Following the original Sensol, the product range expanded to serve different training needs and budgets.

Smart Series

RS02 / RS02 PRO / RS03 / RS03 PRO

Full digital weight technology with 0.5 kg precision increments, robotic arms (0–145°), AI-driven coaching, and complete app integration. This series represents Sensol's most complete engineering.

Flex Series

FLEX AIR / FLEX / FLEX PRO

Mechanical resistance system with the same Sensol build quality and design philosophy. Three tiers with resistance levels from 25 kg to 37 kg, offering a more accessible entry point without compromising on materials or construction.

Every product in the range shares the same foundation: steel and zinc alloy construction, motors built for daily use, and a design standard that earns its place in the home.

04 — Three Years in the US Market

A sustained presence

Since 2023, Sensol has established a sustained presence in the United States through direct sales on Amazon and partnerships with vetted retail channels.

One such partner, Vesta Fitness, carries the Sensol One and Sensol Pro as part of its curated fitness lineup, making the products accessible to customers who prefer purchasing through a trusted American retailer. Over three years, thousands of workouts have been completed on Sensol machines across American homes.

05 — Sensol in Australia

Officially here. Backed locally.

Australia represents one of the world's most active fitness markets. From coastal running culture to the dense network of reformer Pilates studios across Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, and Perth, the demand for premium fitness equipment is well established.

However, Australian consumers purchasing premium smart fitness equipment previously faced international shipping, unclear warranty terms, and support operating in foreign timezones. Sensol is here to change that.

In 2026, Sensol officially launched in Australia. Every machine sold locally comes through the official channel — backed by local warranty, local logistics, and local support.
