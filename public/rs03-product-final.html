What's the warranty?

The frame carries a 10-year warranty covering structural defects. All springs, ropes, wheels, and upholstery carry a 2-year warranty. Full terms are in the owner's manual included with every unit.

Is the RS03 suitable for beginners?

Yes. The 4-spring system lets you adjust resistance from very light (beginner / rehab) to very heavy (advanced). Most beginners start with 1–2 springs and progress as their strength builds.
