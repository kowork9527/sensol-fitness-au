Questions & Answers

Common Questions

How long does shipping take?

We ship within 2-3 business days from our Sydney warehouse. Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days to metro areas across Australia and 7-10 days to NZ. You'll receive a tracking link via email once dispatched.

Do you offer Afterpay?

Yes. We support Afterpay (4 interest-free payments), PayPal Pay in 4, Shop Pay Installments, and all major credit cards. All financing options are visible at checkout.

What's the warranty?

The frame carries a 10-year warranty covering structural defects. All springs, ropes, wheels, and upholstery carry a 2-year warranty. Full terms are in the owner's manual included with every unit.

Is the RS03 suitable for beginners?

Yes. The 4-spring system lets you adjust resistance from very light (beginner/rehab) to very heavy (advanced). Most beginners start with 1-2 springs and progress as their strength builds. We include a Quick Start guide with your first 10 exercises.

What space do I need?

The RS03 is 190 x 64 cm. Allow an extra 30cm of clearance at each end for the carriage travel and rope system. Total recommended space is approximately 250 x 125 cm with ceiling height of 220cm+ for standing exercises.

Can I try it in a showroom first?

We have display units at selected partner studios in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, and Auckland. Contact us at support@sensolfitness.com and we'll arrange a complimentary trial session at the studio nearest you.
