Product Overview

Double the Power.
Twice the Possibility.

The Sensol RS03 represents a generational leap in home fitness technology. Where the RS02 pioneered the concept of a digital weight reformer, the RS03 doubles the motor output from 750W to 1500W per arm, delivering the kind of instantaneous, precise resistance that was previously only possible in commercial studio settings.

With 120 kg of total resistance adjustable in 0.5 kg increments, every movement is measured, controlled, and repeatable.

01 — Digital Weight

0.5 kg precision.
No springs. No guesswork.

Every rep is powered by Sensol's patent-pending digital weight system. Resistance is generated electronically, measured to within 0.5 kg, and adjusted instantly via the Sensol Control Dial™ or the Vesta Sensol App.

No manual spring changes, no stopping mid-set to swap hardware. Just dial it up or down and keep moving. This is the single feature that no competitor offers — even the $15,650 Technogym Reform uses spring resistance.

02 — Expanded Range & Modes

145° of movement.
Three ways to train.

The RS03 features an expanded arm adjustment range of 0–145° across eight positions — a significant upgrade over the RS02's 0–132°. This wider arc unlocks new exercise angles, deeper rotational movements, and lateral training patterns.

Combined with three distinct training modes — Standard (progressive resistance), Off-centre (stabiliser focus), and Constant (uniform tension) — the RS03 adapts to your body, your goals, and your training philosophy.

Pilates reformer.
Strength station.
One machine.

The RS03 is not just a reformer, and it is not just a strength bench. It is both. Switch between Pilates reformer mode and modular strength training mode using the Sensol Control Dial™.

Is the RS03 suitable for beginners?

Absolutely. The digital weight system lets you start as low as 0.5 kg and adjust in 0.5 kg increments — far more granular than spring reformers. The Vesta app includes beginner programmes and Standard mode provides familiar progressive resistance.

The RS03 operates at under 45 dB — quieter than a normal conversation. The motorised system eliminates the clanking and spring noise typical of traditional reformers. Designed specifically for apartment living.

Can I use it for both Pilates and strength training?

Yes — that's the core design principle. Switch between Pilates reformer mode and strength training mode using the Sensol Control Dial™. 200+ movements covering every major muscle group. All accessories included.
