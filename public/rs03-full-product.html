01
1500W Dual Servo Motor — Double the Power

Two 1500W servo motors deliver a combined 3000W of responsive, digital resistance. Exactly double the RS02's 750W output. Faster response times, more consistent tension throughout the entire range of motion, and the ability to handle explosive movements without lag.

vs springs: Traditional reformers like Your Reformer ($2,795) rely on physical springs that degrade over time, offer only 4–5 fixed increments, and cannot be adjusted mid-exercise.

Eight-position arm adjustment from 0° to 145°, a significant upgrade over the RS02's 0–132° range. Unlocks new exercise angles, deeper rotational movements, and lateral training patterns — hip openers, rotational pulls, wide-grip presses.

Digital Weight with 0.5 kg Precision

Every rep is powered by Sensol's patent-pending digital weight system. Resistance is generated electronically, measured to within 0.5 kg, and adjusted instantly via the Sensol Control Dial™ or the Vesta Sensol App. No manual spring changes, no guesswork.

vs springs: No spring reformer in the world offers training modes. Springs behave the way they behave — heavier when stretched, lighter when relaxed.

Training Modes

Three ways to train.
One machine.

Standard Mode

Traditional progressive resistance that increases through the concentric phase. Ideal for building strength and muscle — the familiar feel of weight training, digitised.

Off-Centre Mode

Resistance is biased toward one side of the movement, challenging stabiliser muscles and correcting imbalances. Perfect for rehabilitation and sport-specific training.

Constant Mode

Maintains uniform resistance throughout the entire range of motion, eliminating the slack that springs have at the start and end of each rep. The most biomechanically honest way to train.
