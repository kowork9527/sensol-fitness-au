Questions & Answers

Frequently asked.

Real questions from real buyers. Ask your own — we respond within 24 hours.

Is the RS03 suitable for someone with no Pilates experience?
+
Yes. The Sensol app includes 300+ guided courses ranging from complete beginner to advanced. The Sensol Control Dial™ allows you to start with zero resistance and progress at your own pace. Most beginners complete their first full workout within 30 minutes of setup.
How much space do I need, and does it fold for storage?
+
RS03 occupies approximately 0.2 m² (190cm × 64cm) when in use, and can be stored upright against a wall when not in use. The folded footprint is roughly 64cm × 46cm — small enough to live in an apartment.
What is the difference between RS03 and Sensol One?
+
RS03 is the most advanced model in the Sensol range. It features a higher maximum resistance (45 kg vs 30 kg on Sensol One), the enhanced dual-pulley Pilates cable system, and compatibility with the full Sensol accessory suite including the pull-down bar.
Does RS03 come with a warranty?
+
Yes. The frame carries a lifetime warranty. All other components — including the digital resistance system, carriage, and cables — carry a 2-year comprehensive warranty. We also include a 30-day satisfaction guarantee on the full purchase.
What does the Sensol App include?
+
Every Sensol machine includes complimentary access to the Vesta Sensol App (iOS and Android). The app provides 300+ guided classes across Pilates, strength, yoga, and hybrid training — all led by certified instructors. It also tracks your sessions, resistance progression, and offers real-time form feedback via the machine's Bluetooth connection.
Is assembly required? Can I set it up myself?
+
Minimal assembly required — approximately 15 minutes out of the box. All tools are included. We also offer a white-glove assembly and setup service in metropolitan areas of Australia for an additional fee.
